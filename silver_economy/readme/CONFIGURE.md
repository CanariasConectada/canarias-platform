1. Assign the **Silver Economy User** group to the internal users allowed to
   evaluate their company, and **Silver Economy Manager** to the people who
   manage questionnaires and can override scores.
2. The master questionnaire is created on installation (noupdate data). To
   tune it, open *Silver Economy > Questionnaires* (system administrators
   only) and edit the **Silver Economy** tab of the survey form:
   * score thresholds for Bronze / Silver / Gold,
   * cooldown months after failing, badge validity years, renewal reminder
     days,
   * the microsite *positive items* (question, minimum score, label, icon).
3. Mail templates and daily crons are created automatically and can be
   adjusted under *Settings > Technical*.
