As a merchant: *Website > Configuration > Shop categories*.

Every category of your shop is listed there, your own first, then the shared
ones your products use.

1. Drop the image your shop should show for a category. Only your shop
   changes.
2. *New own category* adds a row for a category that exists on your site
   alone; type its name in the row and file your products under it from the
   product form.
3. Rows of type *Own* can be renamed in the *Name* column and deleted with
   the trash button. Shared rows cannot.

Owners of several shops reach the same screen from the *Categories* button
of *My shops*. Administrators see every shop's tiles grouped by site.
