As a merchant: *Website > Configuration > Shop categories*.

1. Pick a category your products carry and drop the image your shop should
   show for it. Only your shop changes.
2. *New own category* creates a category that exists on your site alone;
   file your products under it from the product form.
3. *Own categories* lists and edits the categories that belong to your shop.

Owners of several shops reach the same screen from the *Categories* button
of *My shops*. Administrators see every shop's tiles grouped by site.
