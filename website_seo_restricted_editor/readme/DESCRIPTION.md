On a multi-site platform a merchant edits their own site as a *restricted
editor*. Odoo shows them **Optimize SEO** on their pages, but saving the
dialog fails: `website.page` is writable by website designers only.

This module lets a restricted editor save the SEO metadata (title,
description, keywords, social image, indexing) of the pages of **their own
company's sites**, and nothing else: no other site, no other field, no
creating or deleting pages. Designers are unaffected.
