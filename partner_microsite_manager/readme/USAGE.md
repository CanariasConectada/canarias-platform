1. Create (or open) the merchant company and make sure it has its own
   website (*Settings > Websites*, one website per company).
2. Open the company form: a **Microsite** tab appears when the company has
   a website.
3. Fill in the content: trade name, hero image, opening hours, delivery /
   parking information, about and services sections, banners, map URL.
   Opening hours use the compact notation
   `L-V 10:00-13:30 / L-V 16:30-20:00 / S 10:00-14:00`
   (`L M X J V S D` = Monday..Sunday, at most two ranges per day).
4. Press **Publish Homepage** once. The homepage of the company website is
   replaced by the dynamic microsite template.
5. From then on, saving the Microsite tab updates the live homepage
   immediately — no re-publish needed.

Logo, favicon and social network links are **not** duplicated here: edit
them on the website itself (*Website > Configuration > Settings*), as
native multi-website already stores them per website.
