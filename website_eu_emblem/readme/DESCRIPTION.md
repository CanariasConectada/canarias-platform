Two institutional marks every public page of the platform has to carry, as
the publicity obligation of the grants that paid for it:

- the EU emblem in the header, right before the cart, on every header variant
  (with an optional funding statement and link set from Website settings);
- the combined FEDER / NextGenerationEU funding strip as the last row of the
  footer, on every page that does not already show it (the auth card and the
  microsite homepages carry their own copy).

The strip is a light band glued to the footer above it, with the image centred
and capped at 1100px, so it reads as part of the footer on the six header and
footer variants in use, and scales down without horizontal scroll on a phone.
