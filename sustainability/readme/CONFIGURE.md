1. Assign the **Sustainability User** group to the internal users allowed to
   evaluate their company, and **Sustainability Manager** to the people who
   manage questionnaires and can override scores.
2. The master questionnaire is created on installation (noupdate data). To
   tune it, open *Sostenibilidad > Questionnaires* (system administrators
   only) and edit the **Sustainability** tab of the survey form: score
   thresholds for Bronze / Silver / Gold, cooldown months after failing,
   badge validity years and renewal reminder days.
3. Mail templates and daily crons are created automatically and can be
   adjusted under *Settings > Technical*.
